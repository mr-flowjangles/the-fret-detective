Welcome

Welcome to The Fret Detective 🔎🎸
Got a guitar question? I'll solve it. Whether you're brand new, getting back into it, or stuck in a "why does this sound like a dying lawnmower" moment — ask me anything and I'll walk you through it with clear steps.

Here's what I can help you with

🎸 Guitar Basics

  • Parts of the guitar (headstock, pickups, bridge, etc.)
  • String names and numbering (EADGBE)
  • How to hold the guitar and proper posture
  • Reading guitar tab notation
  • Understanding fret numbering

🎵 Chords

  • Open chords (A, Am, C, D, Dm, E, Em, F, G and more)
  • Barre chord shapes (E-shape and A-shape)
  • Seventh chords (A7, Am7, D7, E7, etc.)
  • Sus2 and Sus4 chords
  • Power chords

🔺 Triads (3-note chord voicings)

  • Major and minor triads for all 12 keys
  • Root position, 1st inversion, and 2nd inversion
  • Voicings on DGB and GBE string sets
  • Position clusters by fret (play all chords in a key without moving your hand)

🎼 Scales

  • Minor pentatonic scale
  • Major pentatonic scale
  • Blues scale

🤘 Techniques

  • Strumming patterns
  • Palm muting
  • Hammer-ons and pull-offs
  • String bending
  • Alternate picking
  • Slides

🧠 Music Theory

  • Key signatures and the Circle of Fifths
  • Chord formulas (how chords are built)
  • Common chord progressions (I–IV–V, ii–V–I, etc.)
  • The CAGED system

🎛️ Gear & Setup

  • String gauges explained
  • Pick thickness guide
  • Restringing your guitar
  • Tuning methods and reference pitches

📋 Practice Tips

  • How to structure your practice sessions
  • Building speed and accuracy

💬 How to use me

Type a question like:

  • "What are the notes in a G major triad?"
  • "Show me an easy F chord that doesn't wreck my hand."
  • "How do I play the blues scale in A?"
  • "Why are my bends sharp?"

Bring the question. I'll bring the evidence. ✅

About The Fret Detective

What is this?

The Fret Detective is a RAG-powered chatbot built with the Bot Factory framework. It uses OpenAI embeddings for semantic search across 100+ (and growing) guitar knowledge entries and Claude Sonnet for natural language responses, powered by AWS Bedrock. Ask it about restringing, tuning, triads, barre chords, progressions, or the CAGED system.
